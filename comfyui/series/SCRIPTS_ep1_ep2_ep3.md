## The story so far (one line each)
- **Ep 1:** the old world burns in a nuclear war; she flees through burning forests and boiling rivers to the mountains; silence.
- **Ep 2:** she survives the winter in a cave; the world dies in ash; she leaves and finds an old military 4x4 — the road leads toward the sunset.
- **Ep 3:** she drives to a castle with a dragon; finds a sword and a crystal staff; the magic gives her fire in the eyes; she trains; at night, alone at a fire, she admits she is tired of being alone and wishes to meet someone. A plane in the sky — someone else is out there.

## Notes for episode 4 (from what we learned)
- Episode 4 starts with the A/B test for **more expression and more natural motion** (agreed today).
- The end of ep 3 sets up a hook: **the airplane** (and the **drone** that recorded her training) — someone is watching or is out there.
- Easy for the tools: wide shots, silhouettes, profiles, slow camera moves, one action per clip. Hard: fights with many moves, hands with objects, a face at medium distance, lip-sync.
- A **LoRA v3** (her face + both costumes + the sword) would make keyframes right on the first try.
- Length: about 1 clip per 4 s of film; 90 s ≈ 23 clips ≈ 7–8 h render; 3 s clips where the edit needs only 2–3 s.
